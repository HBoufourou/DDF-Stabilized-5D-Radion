# Paper KK — spectral track

**Status:** benchmark passed; real-geometry paper not yet available.

R65 supplies a reproducible validation appendix with three positive controls
and two negative controls. It proves that the diagnostic pipeline can recover
an effectively one-dimensional scalar spectrum under favorable known
conditions.

The scientific paper begins only when R66 supplies a metric approximation on
one real finite DDF geometry. Required evidence includes convergence, mode
profiles, multiplicities, counting law, cap dependence and a transverse gap.

No scalar eigenvalue may be called a graviton KK mass before R67 establishes
the relevant warped spin-2 operator and normalization.
