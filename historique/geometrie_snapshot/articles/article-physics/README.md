# Paper P — physical compactification track

**Status:** blocked until R67–R70.

This folder records the conditions for a possible physical paper. It is not a
manuscript skeleton and contains no assumed target radius.

A paper becomes possible only after one background simultaneously supplies:

- a controlled finite-neck metric and spin-2/KK operator;
- fluxes, branes, tadpole cancellation and quantization;
- a stabilized modulus with positive controlled Hessian;
- a derived hierarchy \(M_{\rm KK}<M_s<M_{\rm Pl}\);
- uncertainty and sensitivity analysis for any predicted length;
- a consistent \(\mathcal N=1\) projection.

Dark matter, dark energy, baryogenesis and the Fano plane remain outside this
paper unless separately derived rather than imported from the historical series.
