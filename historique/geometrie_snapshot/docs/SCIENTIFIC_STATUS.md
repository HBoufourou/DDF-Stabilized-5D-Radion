# Scientific status of corrected DDF

## 1. DDF-G — geometric core

The strongest retained object is the resolved two-component Tyurin family
audited in R63. Its current package includes:

- a projective semistable two-component family;
- smooth quasi-Fano components in the declared DHT sense;
- a smooth K3 seam described as a double cover of
  \(\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1\) branched in bidegree \((4,4)\);
- extendable lattice \(L\simeq U(2)\) under the declared genericity gate;
- rational LMHS data of type \(II_{18}\) under the stated saturation and
  unipotence hypotheses;
- exact computational certificates for the selected toric phase.

This does not by itself prove a long Ricci-flat neck or any physical compactification.

## 2. R64 parity–Hodge result

For a projective two-component Tyurin degeneration with a compatible global
holomorphic involution, standard O3/O7 or O5/O9 truncation, and an equivariant
Clemens tube map with the registered plumbing sign, a surviving active
closed-\(C_4\) eigenclass has a rational orthogonal representative of negative
square.

The result excludes the non-negative-square sector of that precise closed
eigen-tube mechanism. It does not exclude:

- a neutral gravitational KK tower;
- open or relative sectors;
- brane–image dynamics;
- the parent \(\mathcal N=2\) BPS question;
- non-geometric or anti-holomorphic constructions.

The integral tube map and a global tadpole-complete orientifold remain open.

## 3. DDF-KK — spectral branch

R65 validates a diagnostic pipeline on dimensionless controls:

- interval with Neumann caps: passed;
- interval with Dirichlet caps: passed;
- periodic circle with registered doublets: passed;
- transverse contamination: rejected on 5/5 parameters;
- Cheeger/tunneling control: rejected on 4/5 parameters.

The result validates the classifier, not a DDF spectrum. R66 must apply it to
one locked geometry at finite \(t_\star\ne0\). A scalar signal must precede any
spin-2 interpretation.

## 4. DDF-BPS

The parent \(\mathcal N=2\) active lattice can contain non-negative and
isotropic directions, so the parental BPS question remains open. R64 shows
that its standard descent through the tested closed \(C_4\) proper-eigenclass
sector is obstructed. Existence, stability and index non-vanishing are not
established.

- dark matter or dark energy derived from the current compactification;
