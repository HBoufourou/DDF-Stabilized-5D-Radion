# Claim and release policy

## Allowed now

- “We construct and audit a specific resolved Tyurin degeneration.”
- “We obtain a conditional rational parity–Hodge obstruction for a declared
  closed \(C_4\) eigen-tube sector.”
- “A preregistered scalar benchmark distinguishes 1D spectra from two classes
  of false positives.”
- “A neutral KK interpretation remains a hypothesis to be tested on the real geometry.”

## Forbidden now

- “DDF predicts \(R=8.2\,\mu\mathrm m\).”
- “DDF proves a physical KK or BPS tower.”
- “The orientifold compactification is complete.”
- “DDF explains dark matter and dark energy.”
- “The Fano plane organizes seven derived physical charges.”
- “Articles I–VIII are certified or validated papers.”
- “R65 proves the graviton spectrum.”

## Promotion rule

A claim moves from `OPEN` or `CONDITIONAL` to `DERIVED` only when:

1. its hypotheses and normalization are explicit;
2. an executable or analytic certificate exists;
3. counterexamples and scope boundaries are recorded;
4. the claim survives the relevant binary release gate;
5. novelty claims receive a separate literature check;
6. publication claims receive independent human expert review.
