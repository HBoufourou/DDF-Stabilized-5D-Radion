# Disposition of the initial Articles I–VIII

The original papers remain available only for provenance. “Deprecated” means
that the article must not be cited as a validated result of corrected DDF.

| Initial article | Current disposition | Material that may be reused |
|---|---|---|
| I | superseded | motivation and clearly labelled dimensional estimates |
| II | central one-scalar tower interpretation refuted | separate Airy branches as mathematical spectra of their own operators |
| III | quarantined | no physical cascade, dark-matter or RAR claim without a new derivation |
| IV | superseded as evidence | coincidences only as motivation or benchmark |
| V | superseded | computational and variational methods after independent revalidation |
| VI | superseded | moduli-stabilisation questions as an R67–R68 programme; no fixed \(R\) |
| VII | deferred | Fano plane only after seven explicit physical charges and their morphisms exist |
| VIII | conditional archive | baryogenesis mechanism only after a complete background and normalization |

## Citation rule

The historical Zenodo record and GitHub repository may be cited as archived
research material, but not as evidence that the eight physical conclusions
are established. Any future paper must cite the corrected claim ledger beside
the historical record.
