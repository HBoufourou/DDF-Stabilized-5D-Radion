# Publication plan replacing the eight-paper series

The corrected programme uses at most three papers. Their titles are working
titles, not submission-ready manuscripts.

## Paper G — explicit Tyurin geometry

**Working title:** *A resolved toric Tyurin degeneration with K3 seam and a conditional parity–Hodge tube obstruction*

**Retained base:** R63 plus the bounded R64 proposition.

**Submission gate:**

- close the sixteen local ODP models and simultaneous crepant resolution;
- establish or explicitly weaken the R63 coefficient-dominance/genericity claim;
- compare phase, Stanley–Reisner data, cubic form and \(c_2\cdot D\) with known \((5,85)\) models;
- complete the collision search for the relative fan and restriction map;
- state the tube result rationally unless its integral construction is supplied;
- produce a complete LaTeX manuscript and bibliography;
- obtain one K3/toric and one orientifold expert report.

**Current status:** substantial development dossier, not submission-ready.

## Paper KK — finite-neck spectral geometry

**Working title:** *Spectral diagnostics for finite Tyurin necks and effectively one-dimensional modes*

**Retained base:** R65 is the validated benchmark appendix.

**Submission gate:**

- R66 locks and computes one real geometric platform at finite \(t_\star\);
- at least two metric approximations/resolutions agree;
- ten or more modes form a stable 1D packet;
- eigenfunctions extend along the neck rather than localize on caps;
- transverse and tunneling alternatives are quantitatively rejected;
- the scalar result is not mislabeled as spin-2;
- robustness to caps, measure and warping is documented.

**Current status:** method validated, scientific application absent.

## Paper P — physical compactification and scale

**Working title:** *A stabilised Type-IIB finite-neck compactification and its KK hierarchy*

**Submission gate:**

- R67 supplies the physical normalization and operator dictionary;
- R68 derives a stabilized radius without fixing the target by hand;
- tadpoles, flux quantization, branes and hierarchy are complete;
- R69 separates the BPS and KK claims;
- R70 selects one globally consistent \(\mathcal N=1\) architecture;
- any micron value is an output with uncertainty and sensitivity analysis.

**Current status:** blocked research programme. This paper may never exist if
one of the physical gates fails.

## Why there are not eight replacement papers

Splitting one incomplete chain into eight manuscripts created repeated
dependence on the same unproved identifications. Consolidating into three
tracks ensures that each paper has one primary object, one evidence chain and
one falsifiable gate.
